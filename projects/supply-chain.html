PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding Supply Chain Performance

This project involved building an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore different aspects of supply chain performance.

The dashboard brings together business and operational information to provide insights into revenue, products, costs, profitability, suppliers, inventory, shipping, and product quality.

The project helped me strengthen my ability to transform business data into interactive visual reports that can support analysis and decision-making.

Project Focus

  • Supply chain performance
  • Revenue analysis
  • Product analysis
  • Supplier analysis
  • Profitability
  • Inventory and stock levels
  • Product quality

OBJECTIVE

What I Wanted to Analyze

Revenue

Examine revenue across different products, customer demographics, and other business dimensions.

Profitability

Analyze costs and profit margins to understand financial performance across products and suppliers.

Products

Explore product performance, sales activity, stock levels, and product categories.

Suppliers

Examine supplier performance and compare supplier characteristics with profitability and operational data.

ANALYSIS

Dashboard Analysis

Revenue & Sales Analysis

The dashboard provides an overview of revenue and product sales, allowing performance to be explored across different customer and product dimensions.

  • Revenue performance
  • Products sold
  • Revenue by demographics
  • Revenue by product type
  • Revenue by shipping carrier

Cost & Profitability Analysis

Cost and profitability measures were incorporated into the dashboard to help examine how products and suppliers contribute to overall financial performance.

  • Cost analysis
  • Profit margin
  • Profitability by product
  • Supplier profitability analysis

Inventory & Stock Analysis

Inventory-related metrics were used to examine stock levels and identify patterns in product availability and supply chain activity.

  • Stock-level analysis
  • Product availability
  • SKU-level analysis

Product Quality Analysis

The dashboard also includes analysis of product defects, providing another perspective on supply chain and product performance.

  • Defect-rate analysis
  • Product-level quality analysis
  • Comparison of operational performance
